More than 4,000 Walgreens pharmacy locations will remain open under regular business hours on July 4, 2026, one of the busiest days of the year for emergency and urgent care visits. Customers can use 1-hour Delivery and 30-minute Pickup for last-minute essentials, and veterans, active-duty service members, and their immediate families get a 20% in-store discount July 3-5 with valid military ID and myWalgreens enrollment.
